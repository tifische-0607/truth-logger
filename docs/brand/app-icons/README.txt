FA PORTAL PRODUCT MARK: LENS (selected option 6, proposal derived from FridayAnalytics Brand v2.0)
Concept: the FA icon system (gradient tile, Deep Indigo glyph, rising bars) with a magnifier around the bars: search, investigation and the Research module.
Colours: Signal Blue #4A7DFB to Insight Violet #6E59EA gradient, Deep Indigo #252A5C glyph. "FA Portal" in Space Grotesk Medium, converted to outlines.
It does not replace the FridayAnalytics logo. The company logo stays in headers; the product mark is for the app icon, favicon and next to the product name.

svg/       icon (full colour, reverse, mono black, mono white), full-bleed square, horizontal lockups (for light bg, reverse, mono black, mono white), small-size icon
png/       icon 512 and 1024, lockups 800 and 2400 wide
favicon/   favicon.ico (16/32/48, uses the small-size drawing), favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon 180, PWA icons 192/512 and maskable 192/512,
           safari-pinned-tab.svg, mstile-150, site.webmanifest, browserconfig.xml, head-snippet.html
ios/       all iPhone and iPad sizes and the 1024 App Store icon (opaque, no transparency), Contents.json for Xcode
android/   res/ folders: legacy and round launcher icons, adaptive icon (foreground, gradient background, monochrome for themed icons) for mdpi to xxxhdpi, Play Store 512
desktop/   Windows .ico, macOS .icns, PNGs 16 to 1024

Notes
- At 16 px the favicon reads as a magnifier; the bars show from 32 px.
- Adaptive Android icon keeps the glyph inside the 66 dp safe zone.
- Minimum sizes follow the brand pack: icon 16 px / 5 mm, horizontal lockup 120 px / 30 mm.
- Needs brand-owner sign-off before public release.
